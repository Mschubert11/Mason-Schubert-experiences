My Journey

I’m Mason Schubert, a senior studying marketing at Iowa State University. This website showcases my academic experiences, professional development, and the competitive mindset that drives me both inside and outside the classroom. I’ve always believed that success mirrors sports: preparation, discipline, teamwork, and the ability to perform under pressure.

Throughout my time at Iowa State, I’ve built a strong foundation in marketing strategy, analytics, consumer behavior, and digital communication. My coursework and hands‑on projects have strengthened my ability to think critically, solve problems creatively, and communicate effectively. Whether I’m developing a campaign, analyzing data, or collaborating with a team, I bring a high‑energy, sports‑driven mentality to every challenge.

This site highlights key areas of my journey — from my background and interests to the skills I’ve developed and the professional experiences that have shaped my career goals.
